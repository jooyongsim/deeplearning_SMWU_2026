## Lab Development Plan

The 2026 lab sequence is designed around two connected tracks:

### Track A — Core Deep Learning

Most of the foundational practical material will be adapted with minimal changes from the 2025 course repository:

- Optimization and ANN
- PyTorch neural-network modules and training
- CNNs and transfer learning
- Additional vision / representation-learning material where useful
- Generative modeling and Transformer fundamentals

### Track B — Physical AI Extensions

New or substantially expanded 2026 labs will connect deep learning to physical systems:

- Physics modeling with state variables and equations of motion
- Numerical simulation of dynamical systems
- Human demonstration data collection
- Behavior cloning
- Feedback control and reinforcement learning
- Deep RL in physics simulation
- Physics-inspired / physics-informed learning
- Diffusion-based action generation and diffusion policy concepts
- Multimodal AI, VLMs, VLAs, and modern Physical AI case studies

## Project Flow

The project is integrated into the second half of the semester:

1. **Week 7:** Select a project topic.
2. **Week 8:** Submit / present the project proposal.
3. **Weeks 9–13:** Develop modeling, learning, simulation, or control components.
4. **Week 14:** Project milestone and feedback.
5. **Week 15:** Final presentation, simulation/demo, and final deliverables.

Students are encouraged to choose a project that connects deep learning with a physical or simulated system whenever possible.
