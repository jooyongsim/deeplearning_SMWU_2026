## Course Overview

This course introduces the foundations of deep learning and extends them toward **Physical AI**: intelligent systems that connect perception, reasoning, planning, and action with physical systems.

The course begins with core deep-learning topics such as neural networks, backpropagation, gradient-based optimization, regularization, CNNs, and transfer learning. It then moves into dynamical systems, physics-based simulation, behavior cloning, control, reinforcement learning, physics-inspired / physics-informed deep learning, generative models, Transformers, LLMs, multimodal AI, and modern Physical AI topics such as VLM/VLA systems.

Hands-on work is primarily implemented in **Python, PyTorch, and Google Colab**. Students will practice vision-based deep learning, physics simulation, data-driven dynamics modeling, imitation learning, reinforcement-learning-based control, and generative modeling.

## Relationship to the 2025 Course

The 2026 practical sessions will reuse and update much of the material from the [2025 Deep Learning repository](https://github.com/jooyongsim/deeplearning_SMWU_2025). The core deep-learning labs will remain close to the 2025 versions so that students can focus on the underlying concepts, while the 2026 course adds a substantial **Physical AI track** covering physics, simulation, behavior cloning, control, reinforcement learning, physics-informed learning, diffusion policy, and modern multimodal Physical AI.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of the course, students should be able to:

- Explain and implement neural networks, backpropagation, loss functions, and gradient-based optimization.
- Train and regularize deep neural networks using PyTorch.
- Apply CNNs and transfer learning to vision problems.
- Represent physical systems using state variables, equations of motion, and dynamical-system models.
- Implement numerical simulation of physical systems.
- Collect demonstration data and train behavior-cloning policies.
- Understand feedback control, reinforcement learning, and deep RL for physical systems.
- Apply physics-inspired and physics-informed deep learning.
- Understand and implement autoencoders, VAEs, diffusion models, and diffusion-policy concepts.
- Understand Transformer, LLM, multimodal AI, VLM, and VLA fundamentals.
- Integrate deep learning with modeling, prediction, and control of physical systems in a final project.
